# Code of conduct

bhote follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and experience, give and take
criticism of code and ideas, never of people. Harassment, insults, sexualised language or images, and publishing others'
private information are not acceptable, in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere you represent the
project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, contact the maintainers privately: Florian Kappert
([@floriankappert](https://github.com/floriankappert)) or Jakob Beyer ([@jakobbeyer](https://github.com/jakobbeyer)).
Every report is handled confidentially. The maintainers decide on the consequences following the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines, from a private note up to a ban from the project.
